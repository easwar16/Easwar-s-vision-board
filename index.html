The Embarrassment Audit

Not the reasonable reason. The one that makes me sound scared or weak. Then the nicer label I've been giving it.

Work and career

The most embarrassing reason I'm still at Fusion is that I'm scared of losing the job and my parents having to carry my debt, and they can't. Dad is retired. Mom is still working herself tired at TIDCO. Now I'm on a PIP: tasks reopened on my name, noisy calls, and work I let slide while I spent my time and energy on Kasi, who then stopped paying me.I've been calling it building something on the side. It's taking a secure job for granted while chasing someone who didn't value my work.

Money

The most embarrassing reason I'm ₹12.5 lakh in debt is that I'm an impulse buyer. The MacBook and the iPhone earned their place. The Bose headphones, the Samsung watch and the rest of the tech didn't. I bought them before I asked if I needed them.I've been calling it upgrading my life. It's buying the feeling of having made it before I've made it.

Body and health

The most embarrassing reason I don't have the body I want is that I've become lazy and unserious about it. Smoking, drinking and partying on repeat. Underneath, the fat kid still believes this is just how I'm built.I've been calling it enjoying my twenties. It's choosing tonight's relief over the body I keep saying I want.

Relationships

The most embarrassing reason I keep saying yes to the friends who drain me, and kept saying yes to Kasi, is that I'm scared I'll lose them if I say no.I've been calling it loyalty. It's fear of being left out, and it's why Kasi got my work without paying for it.

Read it aloud. Three times.
Performative, uncomfortable, true.
The pattern underneath
Impulse buying→Debt→Stuck at Fusion→Mom keeps working→Stress, late nights→Impulse buying

The loop starts and ends with money. That is why getting debt-free sits at the centre of everything else. Break the buying, and the debt clears. Clear the debt, and every other change stops waiting.

The Environment Audit

An unorganized environment is the consequence of an unorganized mind. Not a wishlist. Every line happens this week.

Layer
Remove
Add
Physical
Any cigarettes beyond today's five. The phone on the bed at night. Coffee after 1 PM.
Only five cigarettes in my pocket each morning. Phone charging away from the bed. Gym clothes laid out the night before. A big steel water bottle that comes everywhere, including the car.
Digital
Saved cards and "No-cost EMI" on Amazon and Flipkart. Shopping apps off the home screen. Instagram and YouTube in the first hour after waking and the last hour before bed.
A headset with mic and a quiet corner for every Fusion call. A wishlist with a 48-hour rule instead of the buy button. A debt tracker updated every payday. Daily limits on Instagram and YouTube.
Input
Work for Kasi until there's a written agreement and payment upfront. Weeknight plans with the friends who drain me. Reels as default rest.
One person a month who is already doing AI consulting, someone I meet, message or study. A fixed weekly slot for Scarbloodtail. Long-form YouTube that teaches the agency business.
Food + body
Drinking on regular nights. Late nights out on workdays. Plates that are mostly rice or roti with little protein.
Protein at every meal: more dal, paneer, curd at home, and eggs or non-veg when eating out. The daily protein shake. Water through the day, not just at night. A cool shower before bed to bring the heat down.
The wake-up shift · 10:30 AM to 6 AM

Don't jump straight to 6. Move the alarm 30 minutes earlier every three days, and go to bed 30 minutes earlier with it. Last coffee before 1 PM. In about four weeks, 6 AM stops being a fight.
